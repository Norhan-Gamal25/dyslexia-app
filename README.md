> <br/>Verbix is an innovative Android application designed to support children with learning disabilities, particularly focusing on dyslexia which affects [5-10% of children worldwide](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6099274/#:~:text=Given%20that%20an%20estimated%205,and%20informed%20understandings%20of%20dyslexia.). The app utilizes advanced OCR and speech recognition technology to analyze handwriting patterns and speech, providing personalized exercises and real-time feedback for improved learning outcomes.

## *Key Features*
### 📝 Handwriting Analysis
- Real-time OCR recognition using Google ML Kit
- Instant feedback on handwriting patterns
- Detailed analysis of letter confusions and missing characters
- Progress tracking and pattern identification

### 🗣️ Speech Pattern Recognition
- Advanced speech analysis for pronunciation patterns
- Real-time feedback on speech accuracy
- Identification of common sound confusions
- Customizable word sets for targeted practice

### ✍️ Interactive Learning
- Touch-based word formation exercises
- Vocal chord word formation exercises
- Personalized practice sessions
- Progress tracking and analytics
- User-friendly interface with engaging visuals

### 📖 AI Storytelling
- AI-generated stories that adapt to the child's reading level
- Stories built around the child's practice words and common trouble letters
- Read-along mode with highlighting to reinforce letter-sound recognition
- Engaging, dyslexia-friendly fonts and spacing

### 👨‍👩‍👧 Parents Dashboard
- Dedicated dashboard for parents to monitor their child's progress
- Overview of writing and speech accuracy over time
- Detailed reports on recurring letter confusions and patterns
- Insights and suggested exercises based on detected patterns

### 🔍 Dyslexia Screening
- In-app screening test to identify potential dyslexia indicators
- Simple, child-friendly assessment questions
- Immediate result summary with recommendations
- A starting point for parents to seek professional guidance

### 🎮 Gamification
- Rewards, badges, and achievements for completing exercises
- Streaks and daily goals to keep children motivated
- Points system tied to accuracy and consistency
- Fun, playful visuals to make learning engaging

### 🃏 Flashcard Recognition
- Point the camera at written flashcards or books
- OCR extracts the words automatically
- Extracted words are added to the child's practice set
- Turns everyday reading materials into personalized exercises

## 👥 Usage

### *Writing Practice*
1. Open the "Practice Writing" section
2. Write the given random paragraph on a page (preferably using a marker)
3. Point your camera at handwritten text
4. Crop the image to remove unnecessary noise
5. Receive instant analysis and feedback
6. Practice suggested exercises

### *Speech Training*
1. Navigate to "Detect Speech Patterns"
2. Speak out the given practice paragraph
3. Record your speech
4. Review detailed analysis and suggestions
5. Practise word-formation at speech training section

### *AI Storytelling*
1. Open the "AI Storytelling" section
2. Let the app pick a story suited to the child's reading level
3. Follow along with read-aloud highlighting
4. Practise target words highlighted within the story

### *Parents Dashboard*
1. Open the dashboard from the main menu
2. View the child's overall progress and accuracy
3. Review detected letter confusions and patterns
4. Get personalized exercise suggestions

### *Dyslexia Screening*
1. Open the "Dyslexia Screening" section
2. Complete the short, child-friendly assessment
3. Review the immediate result summary
4. Follow the recommendations and share with a specialist if needed

### *Flashcard Recognition*
1. Open the "Flashcard Recognition" section
2. Point your camera at a flashcard, book, or any printed text
3. The app reads the words with OCR
4. Words are saved into the practice set for writing and speech exercises
